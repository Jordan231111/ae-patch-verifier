    Install a bundle

    1. Choose the edition for your device and tap Download. If the bundle needs preparation, keep the page open until the .apks download starts.
    2. On your Android device, open the file with a split APK installer that supports .apks bundles.
    3. Follow Android’s prompts to install the complete bundle.
